# Wishflower Privacy Policy

*Last updated 14 September 2026*

Wishflower is a private app for the families of Wishflower Playschool in San Diego. The school's teacher shares photos and a short note about the day, and the parents of enrolled children can see them. This page explains what the app keeps, why, and who can see it.

## Who can use the app

Only families the school has invited, and the school's own teachers. There is no public sign-up. Your account is created by the school and tied to your name and email address.

## What we keep

- **Your name and email address**, so the school knows who is in the app and so you can sign in and reset your password.
- **Photos and videos** the teacher shares, with the day they belong to and any caption.
- **Notes** you leave for the teacher on a day.
- **Notices and documents** the school posts for families — a note, sometimes with a flyer, and files like the handbook or the snack menu — and any reply you leave on a notice.
- **Your choices**, such as whether you asked to be told when there is something new.
- **A record that your phone has signed in**, so the school can see which families are using the app and remove access if needed.

If you turn on notifications, your phone gives us a token that lets us send you one. That token is kept only for as long as you leave notifications on, or until you sign out.

## What we do not do

- We do not sell, share, or rent anything to anyone.
- We do not show ads and we do not use advertising or analytics services.
- We do not track you across other apps or websites.
- Notifications never contain a photo or a child's name.

## Where it is kept

Everything is stored in the United States on Amazon Web Services, in private storage that only the app can reach. Photos and videos are never public on the web. The app shows each photo through a short-lived private link that expires within the hour. All traffic is encrypted.

## Who can see what

- **Families** see the photos, notes and captions the teacher has shared, and can leave notes of their own. Every family sees the same album.
- **The school** sees everything above, plus the list of families using the app, and receives an email when a family leaves a note.
- **Nobody else.** Apple, Amazon and Expo (which delivers notifications) operate the services the app runs on, but they do not receive the photos or notes as content.

## Your choices

- You can turn notifications on or off at any time under *Your account*.
- You can sign out at any time, which removes your phone from the app.
- You can delete a note or a reply you left.
- To remove your family from the app entirely, or to have your data deleted, ask the school. Access is removed the same day.

## Keeping photos

Photos are of the school's children. Please keep any photo you save to your own phone within your family.

## Children

The app is for parents and teachers, not for children, and children do not have accounts.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes, and the school will tell families in the app or by email.

## Child safety

Our standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation, and how to report a concern, are on the Wishflower child safety page: https://simeongbolo.github.io/wishflower-privacy/child-safety.html
