Wishflower Child Safety Standards

Last updated 31 October 2026

Wishflower Playschool is a private app for the families of Wishflower Playschool, a preschool in San Diego. The school's teacher shares photos of the children's day, and only the invited parents of enrolled children can see them. Because the app holds photos of young children, protecting them is the first thing it is built around. This page sets out our standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), and what we do if we ever learn of it.

Zero tolerance

Wishflower Playschool does not tolerate child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or any content or behavior that sexualizes, grooms, exploits, or endangers a child. This applies to every photo, video, caption, and note in the app, and to every person who uses it, including the school's own staff.

How the app protects children

  • No public access. There is no public sign-up and nothing in the app is visible on the web. The school creates every account itself, for the parents of enrolled children and for its own teachers.
  • Children have no accounts and cannot be contacted through the app. There is no private messaging between users.
  • Only the school posts photos. Parents can leave short notes on a day; they cannot upload photos or videos.
  • Photos stay private. They are kept in private storage and shown only through links that expire within the hour. Notifications never contain a photo or a child's name.
  • The school removes access the same day a family leaves the school, or whenever it has a concern about an account.

How to report a concern

Anyone in the app can report a concern from Your account → Report a concern, which writes to the school directly. You can also email undergroundish@gmail.com. Reports are read by the school and treated as urgent and confidential.

If a child is in immediate danger, call 911. You can also report child sexual exploitation directly to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children at report.cybertip.org or 1-800-843-5678.

What we do when we learn of CSAE

  • We remove the content from the app as soon as we know of it.
  • We remove the account involved and preserve what the law requires us to preserve.
  • We report child sexual abuse material to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), as US law requires, and cooperate with law enforcement.
  • We tell the affected families, consistent with the law and any investigation.

Compliance with child safety laws

Wishflower Playschool complies with applicable child safety laws, including US federal law on the reporting of child sexual abuse material (18 U.S.C. § 2258A).
